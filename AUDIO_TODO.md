# Audio needed

Phase 1 has no sound. From Phase 2 the game plays synth placeholders and shows voice lines as on-screen
text until these files exist. Formats: OGG (Opus) plus M4A (AAC) for Safari, effects under 40 KB each.
Write every source and licence into a licences file before adding it.

- Effects: whoosh L/M/H, hit L/M/H, block, counter-hit, throw, tech, land, jump, dash, knockdown slam, KO,
  super activation, Wahala activation, menu move/select/back, one per special (spec section 7).
- Announcer (Nigerian, energetic): Round 1, Round 2, Final round, Fight!, Oya fight!, K.O.!, Perfect!,
  Time!, You win!, You lose!, Wahala!, Counter!, each fighter's name.
- Voices, per fighter: 3 attack grunts, 2 hit pains, 1 per special, 1 per super, 2 taunts, 3 intros,
  3 win quotes, knockdown, KO scream. Iya: Yoruba and English. Egbon: Lagos pidgin. Chief Priest:
  Yoruba incantations, deep voice. Matchup lines are in spec 6.12.
- Music: one loop per stage, menu theme, select theme, victory sting (Afrobeats / Afro-fusion, 1.5 to 2 min loops).
